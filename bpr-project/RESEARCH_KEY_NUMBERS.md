## KEY FINANCIAL METRICS

### Market Penetration & Savings
- **Off-Market Properties**: 33-50% of high-value properties (£1M+) sold off-market in UK
- **Savings Rate**: 9% average economy on negotiations (Black Brick/MySide)
- **Discount Range**: 14-40% discounts on asking price in case studies
  - Case 1: £10M economy (40% discount) on £25M property → purchased at £15M
  - Case 2: £750k economy (14% discount) on property inquiry

### Commissions & Fee Structure
- **Standard Commission Range**: 5-6% of property value (VGV - Valor Geral de Vendas)
- **UK Buyer's Agent Fees**: 1.0% - 2.5% (average 1.5-2.0%)
- **Black Brick Premium Rate**: 2.0-2.5%
- **MySide Split**: 40-50% of gross commission (covering marketing, tech, operations)
- **Personal Shopper (Broker)**: 50-60% of gross commission
- **Autonomous Broker**: 70-80% if self-employed; 30-40% if CLT/associate

### Revenue Models & Pricing
- **Family Office Retainer**: R$ 5k-15k/month OR 0.5-1% of assets under management (AUM)
- **Success Fee (Outcome-Based)**: 3-5% of purchase value
  - Alternative: 20% of negotiation savings/discount achieved
- **Success Fee Range (Alternative)**: On discount obtained (e.g., 20% of economy generated)

### Earnings Potential
- **Promotional Target**: R$ 10k - R$ 40k/month for brokers
- **High-end Property Prices**:
  - Inside Península units: ~R$ 1,350,000
  - Premium neighborhoods: R$ 13,500 - R$ 14,500/m² (projected)
  - Estate range: R$ 80-150 million (family offices/ultra-high-net-worth)

### Revenue Projections (First Year)
- **Initial Investment**: R$ 800k-1.5M (lean structure)
  - Covers: payroll, infrastructure, technology
- **Year 1 Revenue Range**: R$ 2.4-7.5 million (conservative estimate)
- **Total Capital for Operation**: R$ 13 million mentioned

## BUSINESS MODEL COMPARISONS

### MySide (Brazil - 100% Buyer-Focused)
- **Remuneração**: 3-5% success fee on purchase value
- **Commission Split**: 40-50% to MySide operations, 50-60% to broker
- **Technology Stack**: Robust (quoted as key differentiator)
- **Investment**: R$ 800k-1.5M initial
- **Year 1 Potential**: R$ 2.4-7.5M revenue
- **Off-Market Rate**: 50% off-market acquisitions
- **Savings Achievement**: 9% average economy

### Black Brick (UK Premium)
- **Fee Structure**: 2.0-2.5% of property value
- **Case Track Record**: Consistent savings exceeding discounts on asking price
- **Success Rate**: <10% fall-through (vs. 30% market average)
- **Negotiation Approach**: Aggressive, time-pressure based tactics
- **Case Example**: £750k savings on single transaction (14% economy)

### Matchpoint (Comparative Baseline)
- **Operational**: Since 2007, 500+ clients
- **Assets Under Management**: 60%+ allocation across portfolio
- **Real Estate Holdings**: 1 million square meters of properties
- **Service Range**: Comprehensive portfolio management

### Family Office Model (Recurring Revenue Focus)
- **Revenue Structure**: Based on recurring retainers and asset management fees
- **Fee Basis**: 0.5-1% of AUM (annual) or R$ 5k-15k/month
- **Target Client**: HNWI (High Net Worth Individuals) with multiple properties (5-50+ assets)
- **Revenue Stability**: Reduces volatility vs. transaction-only model

## STRATEGIC INSIGHTS

### Market Opportunity
1. **Off-Market Advantage**: 33-50% of premium properties sell off-market, creating exclusive pipeline advantage
2. **Savings Potential**: 9% average economy achievable through specialized negotiation
3. **Recurring Revenue**: Family office model provides stable, recurring revenue (0.5-1% AUM)

### Competitive Positioning
1. **Fee Structure**: 3-5% success fee aligns incentives with client outcomes
2. **Technology Stack**: Robustness mentioned as key differentiator
3. **Scalability**: Lean team (R$ 800k-1.5M investment) can generate R$ 2.4-7.5M Year 1

### Efficiency Metrics
1. **Fall-Through Reduction**: 30% (market) → <10% (best practice)
2. **Sales Force Optimization**: Filtering "warm" leads to reduce expensive sales team time
3. **Process Timeline**: 120-day mandates create clear engagement windows
